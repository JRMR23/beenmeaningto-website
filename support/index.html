Restore Pro

  1. Use the same Apple ID you bought Pro with
  2. Open Been Meaning To and go to Settings
  3. Tap Restore purchases

Billing and refunds

Apple handles all payments. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For refunds, use Apple's refund page.
