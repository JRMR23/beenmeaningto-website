Privacy policy

Your books are your business.

The short version

Been Meaning To does not collect, store or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you add to the app stays on your device.

This policy covers the Been Meaning To app for iPhone, made by Jordan Robinson in the United Kingdom ("we", "us").

What stays on your device

Everything you put into Been Meaning To is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app's own storage. We never receive a copy. This includes:

  • the books you add, with their covers, genres, ratings and progress
  • your reading log, streaks, timer sessions and badges
  • quotes and notes you save
  • your settings, such as your reading goal, colour palettes and reminder time

Been Meaning To's home screen widgets read the same data from a shared space on your device, so they can show your streak and current book. Nothing leaves your phone to make this work.

Looking up books

To find a book's details, cover, genre, publication year and blurb, Been Meaning To sends a search directly from your phone to these free book services:

ServiceRun byWhat it receives
Open LibraryInternet ArchiveBook title, author or ISBN
Apple Books searchAppleBook title and author
Google BooksGoogleBook title, author or ISBN

These searches contain only book details, never your name or anything that identifies you. Like any internet request, each service can see your device's IP address, and its own privacy policy applies. We don't see or keep these searches. Covers that are found are downloaded and saved on your device.

Camera, photos and notifications

Been Meaning To only uses these when you choose to, and they all work on your device.

  • Camera: used to scan a book's barcode and to capture text from a page for a quote. The image is read on your phone and isn't saved or sent anywhere; only the barcode number or the text you choose is kept.
  • Photos: if you pick your own cover art, that image is saved inside the app on your device.
  • Notifications: reading reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

You can turn camera, photo and notification access on or off at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

Been Meaning To Pro and the Style Pack are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your card details, name or Apple ID. The app only receives confirmation of which purchases are active, so it can unlock them. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your iPhone's Settings, and Apple's privacy policy covers the payment itself.

Sharing

When you share a reading card or a palette, the image or text is made on your device and goes only where you send it, such as Messages or Instagram. Those apps' own privacy policies apply once it's shared.

Children

Been Meaning To is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you're always in control of it. Deleting Been Meaning To from your iPhone deletes everything it stored. You can also clear your reading history from Settings in the app. If you use Back up my library in Settings, the backup file is saved wherever you choose, such as iCloud Drive or Files, and only you have it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you have rights over personal data that organisations hold about you. As we hold none, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a concern, you can contact us, or complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this policy

If Been Meaning To changes how it handles data, for example by adding iCloud sync or shared reading circles, we'll update this policy and the date at the top before that feature is released.
