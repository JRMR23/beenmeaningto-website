Spin the shelf

Can't choose? Let the shelf pick.

Stuck staring at a pile of unread books? Tap Spin! and Been Meaning To picks your next read for you. Narrow it down first: anything, something under 300 pages, or a genre you fancy.

  • Scan a barcode or search to add books in seconds
  • Drag your shelf into any order you like
  • Filter by genre or "under 300 pages"

  • A yearly goal that tells you if you're on pace
  • A gentle evening nudge, only on days you haven't read
  • Charts for pages per week, genres and pace (Pro)
