FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

The things people ask most. Can't find yours? Get in touch.

Is Been Meaning To free? +

Yes. Tracking books, logging pages, spinning the shelf, streaks, levels and badges are all free. Pro adds extras like widgets, the lock screen timer, reading charts, Reading Wrapped, quotes and every colour palette. The yearly plan starts with a free week, so you can try it first.

Do I need an account? +

Nope. There's no sign-up and no login. Open the app and start adding books.

Where are my books stored? +

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. We never receive a copy. Read more in our privacy policy.

Which iPhones does it work on? +

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

How does "Spin the shelf" work? +

Tap Spin! and Been Meaning To picks a random book from your to-be-read shelf. Pick a mood first, like a genre or "under 300 pages", to narrow it down. Start the book it picks and you'll earn the Fate's pick badge.

What happens if I miss a day? +

You earn a streak freeze every time your streak hits another seven days, and you can save up to two. If you miss a day, a freeze steps in and keeps your streak alive. You can also turn on a gentle evening reminder that only shows up on days you haven't read yet.

How do I add books? +

Scan the barcode on the back, or search by title or author. Been Meaning To looks the book up on Open Library, Apple Books and Google Books and fills in the title, author, page count and cover for you. You can also type everything in yourself.

Can I bring my books from Goodreads? +

Yes. On goodreads.com, go to My Books, then Import and export, and export your library. Then import that file into Been Meaning To. The app walks you through it.

Does it work for audiobooks and Kindle? +

Yes. Mark each book as paperback, hardback, Kindle or audiobook. Finish books in three different formats and you'll earn the Mixed media badge.

How do I cancel Pro? +

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Been Meaning To. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back? +

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Been Meaning To, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases.

Will my books move to a new phone? +

Yes, if you move your iPhone over with an iCloud backup or Quick Start, your books come with it. You can also make your own copy: in Been Meaning To, go to Settings, tap Back up my library and save the file somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive. On the new phone, tap Restore from backup and pick that file.

Is there an Android version? +

Not right now. Been Meaning To is made for iPhone.
